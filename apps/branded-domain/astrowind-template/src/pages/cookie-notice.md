## What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies may include local storage, pixels, tags, and scripts.

- keep the website functioning properly
- support security and abuse prevention
- remember basic preferences
- understand high-level usage patterns and improve the site
