# OKRs & Metrics

> Owner: _TBD_ · Status: `DRAFT` · Last reviewed: 2026-07-07

What "winning" looks like this stage, measured. Keep it small — at pre-first-go-live, most
vanity metrics are noise. One north star, a few leading indicators.

---

## North star (right now)

> **First operating business moving real money in production, with the ledger tying out.**

Not revenue, not sign-ups, not traffic. Until this is done, it is the only score that matters.
It maps to the product's go-live exit criterion — now on the **Rapyd** rail (Airwallex dropped
on cost; the codebase AW1–AW3 milestones are deprecated from the GTM path).

## Leading indicators (track weekly)

These predict the north star before it happens:

- **Fit conversations held** (founder discovery calls).
- **Pain confirmed rate** — % of conversations where merged-truth pain is real and urgent.
- **Design partners scoped** — flows ready to switch on at go-live.
- **Regulatory blockers open** — count trending to zero.

## Lagging indicators (track once live)

Don't obsess over these until Phase 3+:

- Customers live (real money moving).
- Money moved through the platform.
- Revenue (base + usage).
- Reference customers / case studies.
- Net revenue retention / expansion.

## Funnel metrics (once there's a funnel)

Track conversion across the [GTM funnel](../04-gtm/gtm-strategy.md#the-gtm-funnel-define-the-stages-well-actually-track):
Fit → Conversation → Design partner → Live → Reference → Expansion. Watch where deals stall.

## Anti-metrics (do NOT optimize for these now)

- Website traffic, social followers, waitlist size — vanity at this stage.
- Feature count — the product roadmap owns this; BD doesn't win by asking for more features.
- Number of segments touched — focus beats breadth pre-proof.

## Review cadence

- **Weekly:** leading indicators + pipeline next steps.
- **Monthly:** OKR progress; re-check phase gate vs. [`../../roadmap/`](../../roadmap/).
- **Quarterly:** set the next OKR; retire what's stale.
